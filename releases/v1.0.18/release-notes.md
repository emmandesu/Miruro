## New features
- Downloads now has its own entry in Miruro’s main menu.
- Queue up to 20 episodes and download them one at a time.
- See progress, percentage and downloaded size for each active episode.
- Tap × to cancel an active or waiting download.
- Completed episodes stay listed. Tap one to choose a video player with Android’s Open with window.
- Clear finished history without deleting your saved videos.

## Improvements
- Downloads matches Miruro’s menu and settings design.
- Each transfer uses the DNS provider selected when it starts.
- Saved-file access is retained across app restarts when the selected folder supports it.
- Interrupted and failed downloads show a useful status. Missing files and video players are handled gracefully.
- Existing popup, redirect and MP4 validation protections remain enabled.

## Notes
- If Android stops the app, prepare fresh links for interrupted downloads; partial transfers do not resume.
- Files saved with older versions remain in their chosen folders, but cannot be automatically added to the new history.
- Install over your current Miruro app to keep your settings and sign-in.
