# Miruro 1.0.30

## New
- Added a slim brightness slider at the far-left edge of the player, in normal and fullscreen viewing.
- Slide up to brighten or down to dim. A sun icon and percentage show the level; tap the sun to restore device brightness.
- The slider fades with player controls, stays visible while dragging, and is disabled when touch lock is enabled.
- Brightness changes apply only inside Miruro. Leaving the player, opening Client Settings, backgrounding or entering PiP restores normal brightness.
- Includes accessibility brightness adjustment and reset. No extra permission is needed.

Signed with the original Miruro certificate. Physical-device brightness and alignment still need confirmation.
