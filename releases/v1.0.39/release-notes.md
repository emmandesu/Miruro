# Miruro 1.0.39

## Playback recovery

- After the connection returns, Miruro gives the video a moment to recover and tries to restart loading once when supported.
- If buffering continues or the stream fails, tap **Retry** on the inline or fullscreen player. If a fresh stream is needed, Miruro reloads the same episode and seeks back to your saved position when the player supports it.
- Paused videos stay paused. Miruro never reloads the whole episode automatically.
