## New feature
- Lock or unlock orientation from the fullscreen player, using a new rotation button above the touch lock.
- Fullscreen starts locked in landscape. Unlock to rotate freely, then lock again to keep the current angle, including portrait or reverse landscape.

## Improvements
- Matching white line icons and translucent round buttons with accessible 48 dp tap targets.
- Rotation button fades with the player controls and returns when you tap the player.
- Touch lock hides the rotation button to prevent accidental changes. Unlock touches first to change rotation.
- Exiting fullscreen restores the previous app rotation setting; each new fullscreen session starts locked in landscape.
- Added a short guide under Client Settings → General → Player.

## Notes
- Install over your current Miruro app to keep sign-in and settings.
- Rotation and fullscreen playback still need testing on a physical phone.
